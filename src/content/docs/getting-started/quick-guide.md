This page covers the essentials for your first session.

## Logging in

campusGenAI uses your institution's Single Sign-On (SSO). Navigate to your institution's campusGenAI URL and sign in with your normal institutional credentials — the same username and password you use for other campus systems.

If you're on the campusLaunch deployment as part of the AI Enablement Program, log in at [hub.campusgenai.org](https://hub.campusgenai.org). If you're at an institution with its own deployment, your IT department or AI lead will have shared the URL. If you're not sure who that is, [contact us](/get-help/).

## The interface

After logging in you'll see the chat interface. The main areas:

- **Model selector**: Top of the screen, lets you choose which AI model to use
- **Chat input**: Bottom of the screen, where you type your messages
- **Sidebar**: Left side, shows your conversation history and the Agents section

## Sending your first message

Click the chat input at the bottom, type a message, and press **Enter**. The AI responds in real time.

Start with something from your actual work — a document to summarize, a draft to review, a question you've been meaning to research. The platform is most useful when you bring real tasks to it.

## Switching models

campusGenAI gives you access to multiple AI models — GPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, and others depending on your deployment. To switch:

1. Click the model selector at the top of the chat
2. Choose a model from the list
3. Your next message uses the selected model

Different models have different strengths. See [Model Comparison](/models/model-comparison/) for details on when to use each one.

## Starting a new conversation

Click **New Chat** in the sidebar (or the pencil icon, depending on your deployment). Each conversation starts fresh — the AI doesn't carry context between separate conversations.

Your past conversations are saved in the sidebar and you can return to any of them at any time.
