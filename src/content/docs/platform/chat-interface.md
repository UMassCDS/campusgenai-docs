## Overview

When you log in to campusGenAI, the main view is the chat interface. It has three primary areas:

- **Model selector** — at the top of the chat, lets you choose which AI model handles your conversation
- **Chat area** — the main content area where your conversation appears
- **Input box** — at the bottom, where you type messages and attach files
- **Sidebar** — on the left, contains your conversation history, the Agents section, and settings access

## Starting a conversation

Type your message in the input box at the bottom and press Enter (or click the send button). The model responds in real time — you'll see the response appear as it's generated.

There's no special format required. You can ask questions, paste in text to work with, give instructions, or start a back-and-forth discussion. The AI maintains context within a single conversation — you can refer back to earlier messages and build on previous responses.

## The model selector

The model selector at the top of the chat controls which AI model processes your messages. Click it to see the models available in your deployment and select a different one.

Switching models takes effect on your next message. The conversation history stays visible, but the new model starts fresh — it doesn't have context from messages sent to the previous model.

See [Model Comparison](/campusgenai-docs/models/model-comparison/) for guidance on choosing between models.

## Managing conversations

**Starting fresh:** Click **New Chat** in the sidebar to start a new conversation. Previous conversations are not affected.

**Returning to a past conversation:** Your conversation history appears in the sidebar. Click any past conversation to return to it. The full context is preserved — you can continue where you left off.

**Conversation context:** Each conversation has its own context window. If a conversation becomes very long, older messages may fall outside the model's context. For long-running projects, it's often better to start a new conversation with a fresh summary of where you are.

## Attaching files

Use the attachment button (paperclip icon) in the input area to upload documents. See [Working with Files](/campusgenai-docs/platform/working-with-files/) for supported formats and how to work with uploaded content.

## Keyboard shortcuts

- **Enter** — send message
- **Shift+Enter** — add a line break without sending
