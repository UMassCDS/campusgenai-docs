## Accessing settings

1. Click your **profile icon** in the bottom-left of the sidebar
2. Select **Settings** from the menu
3. Navigate between tabs for different categories

If your institution uses SSO, some settings are managed by your administrator and won't be editable here.

## Account settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| Display name | How your name appears in the platform |
| Theme | Switch between light and dark mode |
| Language | Interface language (if multiple languages are enabled) |
| Default model | Which model is selected when you start a new conversation |

## Usage and balance

Your credit balance, token usage, and (in some deployments) file-storage usage are visible under **Settings → Balance** or **Settings → Usage**. For how credits and tokens work, refill mechanics, and choosing models for cost, see [Credits & Tokens](/platform/credits-and-tokens/).

## Model access

Not all models are available to all users. Your administrator controls which models are enabled for your account. If you need access to a specific model that isn't showing in your model selector, contact your IT department.

## Notification preferences

Depending on your deployment, you may be able to adjust email or in-platform notifications for activity in shared agents or spaces.

## SSO and authentication

campusGenAI uses your institution's SSO system. You authenticate with your normal institutional credentials. Sessions expire according to your institution's security policy — you may need to re-authenticate periodically. You cannot change your password or primary email directly in campusGenAI; manage those through your institution's identity system.
