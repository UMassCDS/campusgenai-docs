This page covers who can request KeyMaker access, how to request it, and how sponsors provision access for their teams.

## Eligibility

- **Faculty and staff with funding** can request access as a **sponsor**, using a funding source they control.
- **Students** can request access through a sponsor — by joining the team of a faculty or staff member who sponsors them.

## Funding requirements

KeyMaker is self-funded: sponsors bring their own funding source to cover usage costs.

:::caution[Speedtype required]
A valid **speedtype is required at the time of request**. Usage is billed to the speedtype you provide. Have it ready before you start.
:::

## How to request access

In campusGenAI:

1. Click your **profile picture** in the top corner.
2. Select **KeyMaker**.
3. Choose the tab that fits your situation:
   - **Trial Key** — to try out API access.
   - **Sponsor Access** — if you have funding you'd like to use, or you're part of a sponsor's team.

Under **Sponsor Access**, there are two paths depending on who you are:

- **Join your sponsor's team** — choose this if you're being added to an existing sponsor's team (for example, a student working under a PI).
- **Register as a team Sponsor** — choose this if you have funding you'd like to use.

Fill out the information for your path accordingly and submit.

## Approval workflow and timeline

Timing varies depending on verification. After you submit a request:

1. Your **billing admin** verifies that the speedtype is valid. Usage is then manually billed to that department/speedtype accordingly.
2. Your **platform admin** approves key creation.

Once approved, you're ready to retrieve your key.

## How you receive your key

Your API key is **shown in the KeyMaker portal in campusGenAI** — no key is emailed. Return to your profile picture → **KeyMaker** to find it.

:::caution[Treat your key like a password]
Anyone with your key can use models billed to your funding source. Never share it or commit it to a repository. See [security best practices](/keymaker/usage/#security-best-practices).
:::

## Team provisioning (for sponsors)

Once a sponsor request is approved, sponsors manage their team **through the KeyMaker portal in campusGenAI**:

- Add or remove team members
- Allocate access and usage across team members

Team members who chose **Join your sponsor's team** appear for the sponsor to approve and provision.
